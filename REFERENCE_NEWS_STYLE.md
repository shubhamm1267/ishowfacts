The compact black/yellow/white top headline uses a separately proofread 4–7 short word hook ending in one emoji. SVG/resvg shapes Hindi independently of local Pillow RAQM support. Titles, SVG explanations and shaped overlays now use the bundled **Noto Sans Devanagari Bold**, a static 700-weight instance at normal width from the official Google Fonts source; its OFL license accompanies it in `assets/fonts/noto`. Hindi generation requires a successful native copyedit, local encoding/matra checks, and an independent final-copy language/fact review before TTS. Failure no longer silently returns the unchecked draft. Clearly figurative harmless jokes are allowed; unsupported literal consequences are rejected. A whole-narration audio review then compares the actual generated WAV against the expected script. A rejected voice is regenerated once and reviewed again; a second rejection stops generation. Provider outages do not trigger needless voice regeneration. [Gemini audio input](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/audio) supports this check. These are stronger AI quality checks, not a guarantee that automated linguistic judgment is infallible.

Narration direction uses a topic-specific audience question whose answer is actually supplied by the source/script, a quick factual reveal and a brief relatable joke. Energetic delivery uses native Anuj by default, distinct role-dependent speaking rates (1.16–1.29), modest +2-semitone pitch lift, presence EQ and compression. Two-pass normalization targets −8.5 LUFS with a −1dBTP ceiling. The reference measured −6.8 LUFS and +1.72dBTP; the output aims for similar punch without reproducing its overshooting peaks. The older voices remain selectable; balanced delivery keeps its gentler pacing. Pitch matching is approximate and does not clone the reference narrator or repeat its unsupported crash claim.

Reference-style sound design has no default cut whoosh, inferred impact boom or automatically voiced prop. Only explicit cues with a specific search query and a substantive `sfxReason` qualify; at most two cues are scheduled. Strict Freesound search and audio relevance review must pass before use. Bubble queries require bubble-labelled metadata rather than a generic plastic-pop fallback. Cues are quiet and short, aligned by `sfxAt` to a fraction of the relevant narrated shot. If no cue is necessary or a cue cannot pass review, silence under the narration is valid.

Automatic storyboards default to no explanatory diagram. Optional storyboard point-out contract:

```json
{"pointOut":{"target":"visible solar panel","label":"सोलर पैनल","kind":"arrow","at":0.3}}
```

`kind` can be arrow, circle or underline; `at` is a fraction of shot duration. Arrows end in an arrowhead only, without a dot. The storyboard cannot supply trusted coordinates. Multimodal inspection must locate the visible target with confidence >=0.85, then an independent inspection checks an annotated review image to ensure the marker centre is actually on the named feature. Still images get one coordinate; video samples at 12%, 50% and 85% must all pass both stages. Unverified, absent or ambiguous targets omit the pointer. Accepted coordinates interpolate across sampled frames and transform with the same crop, zoom and pan as the source. This is sparse tracking, not a frame-by-frame optical tracker; fast or occluded targets may still be unsuitable and require review.

`studio/lib/reference-style.mjs` creates actual SVG paths and uses `@resvg/resvg-js` to rasterize a 12-frame stroke reveal. The renderer composites these transparent overlays over the verified target, then places the original top headline above everything. SVG sources and animation frames are saved in job assets. No decorative pointer is drawn at a guessed screen location.
