Typography now works through shared `wordGeometry`/`wordSVG` functions in `studio/lib/typography.mjs`. Every Devanagari word is segmented into complete orthographic grapheme clusters with `Intl.Segmenter`: vowels, nukta and conjuncts remain attached. Measured clusters receive a small visible gap between them; words receive a larger gap. This applies to arbitrary titles, shaped labels and SVG explainer text, not a dictionary of words like “होता”. Actual headline raster pixels are checked at runtime for inter-word overlap before the asset is accepted. Tests cover “होता”, “बनाती”, “क्यों”, “पृथ्वी”, “क्षेत्र”, “प्रश्न”, “खुशियाँ” and other words, preserving every original Unicode cluster. Long titles still require rewriting rather than shrinking beyond the readable minimum.
